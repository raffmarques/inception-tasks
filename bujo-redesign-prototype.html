Design decisions
↳ bottom nav
View tabs move from top to bottom. Copper top-line marker on active tab — mirrors the flow column treatment. 4 tabs: focus · flow · lists · migrate.
↳ 44px signifier target
Signifier column widens from 20px to 44px with a vertical divider — isolating the cycle-status tap from the open-detail tap. Meets WCAG 2.5.5 target size.
↳ sidebar → bottom sheet
The W12 sidebar chip at the bottom of the task list opens a sheet. This preserves the passive context of the desktop sidebar behind a single intentional tap.
↳ flow peek → bottom chip
The vertical peek sidebar strip becomes a persistent bottom chip above the nav bar. The up-arrow (↑) signals it expands upward. Period tasks slide up in a sheet.
↳ task detail sheet
Replaces the side panel — slides up to 90% height. Drag handle at top affords swipe-to-dismiss. Fields stack vertically (no 2-column grid on narrow screens).
↳ zoom strip
Zoom pills move below the header as a horizontally scrollable strip with pill-style buttons — no borders, just the active pill fills to ink.
↳ what stays the same
Flow view horizontal scroll is natural on touch. Highlight zone, signifier symbols, typography, and color tokens are identical — mobile is not a redesign, just a layout remap.
